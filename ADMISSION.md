# Language and dependency admission

The candidate forms in the supplied v6.1 document are evidence of desired
capabilities. They are not a fixed instruction set. This audit distinguishes a
necessary capability, a representation of it, and a new primitive. A reduction
must preserve the declared formation boundary as well as truth.

The current positive language has one definition interface, identified clauses,
identified prospective premises, scoped patterns, and complete material
equations. Its monotone consequence operator is fixed independently of proofs.
The native compiler gives every formed finite system a closed structural
representation. The remaining higher-language and foundation audit is open.
