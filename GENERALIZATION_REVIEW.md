# Review of the generalization machinery, 2026-09-10

The machinery has helped expose lost distinctions and verify proposed repairs.
It has not absorbed all repeated reasoning. The most persistent external steps
have been deciding which conflicting observations to withdraw, reassessing
the scope of an apparently successful experiment, choosing a construction when
the available language cannot repair a failure, and auditing the evidence that
connects a finite table to its intended subject. Successful evaluator execution
does not discharge those steps.

This review covers every accessible earlier evaluator result found in the
project artifacts: 165 executions, comprising 141 observation investigations
and 24 inference investigations. There are 157 evaluated inputs and eight
deliberate formation rejections. The [individual run ledger](validation/generalization-review-2026-09-10/RUNS.md)
and [complete finite results](validation/generalization-review-2026-09-10/historical-runs.json)
retain the per-run findings. The inventory contains 78 distinct finite
evaluation states. Repeated states often check changed implementations or
proof contexts; they are not counted as independent content discoveries.
The five completed input-admission runs captured after this inventory, and the
new experiments prompted by this review, are follow-up evidence.

## Repeated reasoning now entering the machinery

Twenty-four well-formed historical runs select conflicting facets. In sixteen,
withdrawing the unsound selected facets exposes additional missing comparisons.
Fourteen have no adequate preserving extension although an adequate available
basis exists. The old report already exposes the whole sound available
language; the missing operation is a justified transition that conserves the
selected sound part and explains new additions after withdrawal.

The explicit counterexample uses two candidates with the intended comparison
`0 <= 1`. Selected facet 0 conflicts with that comparison but already separates
`1` from `0`. Selected facet 2 is sound and constant. Facet 1 supplies a sound
separation; unselected facet 3 is constant. The old report returns no repairs.
After facet 0 is withdrawn, a new failed pair `(1,0)` has a repair through
facet 1. Reusing the original empty repair report fails. Selecting every sound
facet works but adds facet 3 without a new-distinction witness.

[Observation_Revisions](theories/Observation_Revisions.thy) derives the general
transition from existing conflicts and repairs. It preserves selected sound
facets, excludes every selected facet that any adequate available basis must
exclude, and recomputes complete repair witnesses on the retained selection.
The resulting selection is sound and has exactly the failures of the whole
sound available language. It is adequate exactly when an available adequate
basis exists. This is a separate revision proposal; the preserving extension
keeps its original meaning. No minimum or unique selection is claimed.

The executable interface now returns retained facets, withdrawals, recomputed
witnesses, the proposed selection, and its remaining comparisons. Rejected
inputs receive no revision proposal. The first actual counterexample execution
returns retained `{2}`, withdrawn `{0}`, repair `(1,0,1,0)`, and selection
`{1,2}` with no residual.

The runner also retains the exact bytes of external case evidence and execution

Digests identify those bytes; they do not establish the semantic truth of a
case or identify equivalent presentations. A generated, proof-checked export

The owner's subsequent correction identifies the larger missing capability:

## Continuing review rule

Review content quality after every two completed content loops, and sooner
when the same reasoning is performed again or an apparently successful scope
proves too weak. A loop includes the initial evaluated question, its actual
residual and witness evidence, the resulting construction or selection, and a
new evaluation on the same intended subject. Changes of scope or observation
language are recorded explicitly.

At each review, reconstruct the new runs, inspect their subject and evidence
boundaries, and identify repeated judgments that still occur externally. State
an independent requirement for any proposed improvement, compare actual method
candidates using the existing evaluator, prove the reusable rule, then execute
the revised method and follow its returned proposal. Keep unresolved language,
scope, or evidence deficiencies visible. Track the reason each new general
rule improves produced content; elapsed time and the number of passed builds
are not the quality criterion.

The next reviews still owe structured assessment of scope adequacy, separation
of available-language limits from comparison-model limits, and guidance for
constructing observations outside the supplied language. Profile inclusion
itself can only realize a preorder; an arbitrary supplied relation can be
well-formed yet incompatible with that model. The current report does not
automatically diagnose that stronger obstruction. Native presentation of the
whole investigation report, native mathematical-proof admission, the wider
application audit, and the final every-line audit also remain open.
