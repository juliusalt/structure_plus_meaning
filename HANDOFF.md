**Working rules every brief carries** (as the harness stands; what it does itself is not asked of a task):
- **Hand-over.** In a task's own tree the hand-over is its files, the repository's check (`v2.py check`) and its
  `commit.md`, so an Acceptance may say "the repository's check". Batches and trains check the work merged onto main,
  so a task brings main in (`v2.py bring-main`) only when told its lines meet main's. The source checks and the import
  graph are answered at every change that writes a theory; the commit states the harness's record of the session's
  probes, so a `commit.md` Validation paragraph states only what no record does. No session runs `tools/check.py`.
- **Naming.** Every theory a task works on is named in its brief (`theories/X.thy` or `X`) and every fact it consumes
  as `Theory.name`: its session is given their statements and relations as it starts, their size out of its room. A
  Deliverable names files, never a directory; a bare name is the task's own folder's unless the root has that file, a
  new root file is `./NAME`; a brief names its folder `.build/tasks/<id>/`, never an edit's key.
- **Timings.** A timing is held (`v2.py measuring --exclusive`, bounded at 3 minutes) or shared (`--shared`); a brief
  that asks for one says which and sizes a held run to fit. A re-measured quantity states its scale factor and its
  absolutes in both forms; a before/after pair in the same holds is its own reference; a factor from an unrelated
  computation is not a scale; an estimate is compared first in the quantity it counts (q85), and one written before
  the cases were chosen is not a target. A held run times the operation alone, compiled outside the timed seconds; a
  held run's caller compiles the given with the recording before the hold (15.2 s).
- **Probes.** A change that writes a theory says `--probe` to be probed; a probe is an inner loop, not a gate (q26),
  bounded at 60 s unless a measurement holds the machine; its evidence is `probe.summary.json`. An edit to a base
  theory is probed from the tree with the unchanged theories between it and its changed dependents loaded as renamed
  copies, which does not finish within 60 s, so a brief asking for one names what it loads or accepts the repository's
  check for the combination. A probe starts in about 4.3 s on the one-session base and loads its theories in one call
  (#951), so one failing theory certifies none of them; a failed proof that does not return stays silent until the
  60 s kill (#944). A probe from a tree behind main whose base advanced is refused: `v2.py bring-main`, `--base` at the
  heap the tree matches, or `--from-heap` for one theory; a changed theory's `export_code … checking` is blanked and
  named skipped; a past base's sources are read with `--base-sources`.
- **Evaluations.** A theory that evaluates constructions evaluates them in one evaluation, compiled once (each `value`
  or `@{code}` compiles the term's whole closure, about 5.5 s, while executing it takes 0.4 s, 0.011 s where
  `Native_Execution_Refinements` is in effect). Evidence to retain is constructed in a recipe whose export includes
  `Native_Execution_Refinements`; a held run pins `--base` at its claim. A library theory on the route runs no
  evaluation and imports no refinement collection; a statement too large for code_simp is discharged by a
  kernel-checked composition down its parts (q93).
- **Receipts and records.** The harness advances the base and retains the receipts at every landing; no task does. A
  brief whose work re-records a recipe's reports or answer records names each file in its Deliverable. Answer records
  are replayed on a base holding their state closures as HEAD has them.
- **Merges.** A task whose commit fails at the merge is queued again as it is; `bring-main` merges main, never a row by
  hand. Where both sides changed one THEORY_MAP.md row, `bring-main` is refused: the round hands over main's row
  verbatim and leaves its change in `rows.md` for a task made from main; where the work changed that theory's imports,
  it moves what needs the new import into a theory above it and sets the theory and its row back (q76). A task in the
  one tree installs its theories only at hand-over and keeps nothing uncommitted there when it parks (q30).
- **Facts in a locale context** reach only the interpretations registered in theories the noting theory imports; facts
  moved above an interpretation are cited as `L.fact[OF <the predicate, stated once>]`; a move of facts probes the
  theories that cite them.
- **Text with backticks goes through a file**: `v2.py reply`, `tell` and `ledger` pass an argument through a shell,
  which strips a backticked name, so such a text is written to a draft and passed with `--file`.
- **Size.** A brief's Size is judged against its room with its own relations; a task beyond its room is handed back at
  its first start and trimmed to the reasoning that belongs together, its corollaries and moves becoming tasks, never
  by dropping what the kept work must respect.
- **Scope.** Small repairs of the same theories and consolidating follow-ups go into one task up to about half its
  room; a repair another task waits on, or one of a notion's contract, stays its own task. Before briefing a task that
  edits a theory, search `.build/plans/next-edits.md` for it. A task that continues one task's work says `continues`.
  A partial task whose deliverables are written is re-planned, not split; no task rests on reasoning not yet written;
  a fix the planner creates carries no review; a design entry narrowed by its implementation gains a marked
  correction; a tidy rides with a substantive change of the same theory, never a change of its own.
- **Rebuilds.** A move into a low theory rebuilds nearly the whole library (1798 of 1897 theories, 1770 s): low moves
  are batched into one task, put at the queue's head while it checks, so that no other heavy run starts beside it.
