**Working rules every brief carries** (as the harness stands since 2026-09-24; what it does itself is not asked of a
task):

- **Hand-over.** In a task's own tree the hand-over is its files, the repository's check (`v2.py check`) and its
  `commit.md`, so an Acceptance may say "the repository's check" without its command. Batches and trains check the work
  merged onto main, so a task brings main in (`v2.py bring-main`) only when told its lines meet main's. The standing
  source checks and the import graph are answered at every change that writes a theory; the commit states the
  harness's record of the session's probes beside its check, so a `commit.md` Validation paragraph states only what no
  record does, never a check's outcome. No session runs `tools/check.py`: it rewrites tracked `validation/` files and is
  the finalizer's (#533's review).
- **Naming.** Every theory a task works on is named in its brief (`theories/X.thy` or `X`) and every fact it consumes
  as `Theory.name`: its session is given their statements and relations as it starts, and their size comes out of its
  room; a theory named in plain text counts as a backticked one. A Deliverable names files, never a directory; a bare
  name is the task's own folder's unless the root has that file, a new root file is `./NAME`; a brief names its folder
  `.build/tasks/<id>/`, never an edit's key.
- **Timings.** A timing is held (`v2.py measuring --exclusive`: every other run stops, bounded at 3 minutes) or shared
  (`--shared`, told the machine's load); a brief that asks for one says which and sizes a held run to fit. A run
  re-measuring a quantity recorded elsewhere states its scale factor and its absolutes in both forms; a before/after
  pair timed in the same holds is its own reference, its scale a control whose code no commit between the two bases
  changes; a factor from an unrelated computation is not a scale; an estimate is compared first in the quantity it
  counts (q85), and one written before the cases were chosen is not a target. A held run times the operation alone,
  compiled outside the timed seconds; a probe of X's execution theory needs about 90 s (#539's review); a held run's
  caller compiles the given with the recording before the hold (15.2 s, `.build/tasks/561/`).
- **Probes.** A change that writes a theory says `--probe` to be probed, and its reply carries the finding; a probe is
  an inner loop, not a gate (q26), bounded at 60 s unless a measurement holds the machine; its evidence is
  `probe.summary.json`. An edit to a base theory is probed from the tree with the unchanged theories between it and its
  changed dependents loaded as renamed copies; such a probe does not finish within 60 s, so a brief asking for one names
  what it loads or accepts the repository's check for the combination (#398's review). The base is one session over
  HOL with a short chain above it, so a probe starts within about 10 s. A probe from a tree behind main whose base
  advanced is refused: `v2.py bring-main`, `--base` at the heap the tree matches, or `--from-heap` for one theory; a
  changed theory's `export_code … checking` is blanked and named skipped; a past base's sources are read with
  `--base-sources`.
- **Evaluations.** A theory that evaluates constructions evaluates them in one evaluation, compiled once: each `value`
  or `@{code}` compiles the term's whole closure (about 5.5 s, #425) while executing it takes 0.4 s, 0.011 s where
  `Native_Execution_Refinements` is in effect. Evidence to retain is constructed in a recipe whose export includes
  `Native_Execution_Refinements`; a held run pins `--base` at its claim. A library theory on the route runs no
  evaluation and imports no refinement collection (#415's review); a statement too large for code_simp is discharged by
  a kernel-checked composition down its parts (q93).
- **Receipts and records.** The harness advances the base and retains the receipts at every landing; no task does. A
  brief whose work re-records a recipe's reports or answer records names each file in its Deliverable, and the landing
  check compares against them (Q12). Answer records are replayed on a base holding their state closures as HEAD has
  them (#143).
- **Merges.** A task whose commit fails at the merge is queued again as it is; `bring-main` merges main, never a row by
  hand. Where both sides changed one THEORY_MAP.md row, `bring-main` is refused: the round hands over main's row
  verbatim and leaves its change in `rows.md` for a task made from main; where the work changed that theory's imports,
  it moves what needs the new import into a theory above it and sets the theory and its row back (#165, q76; Q13). A
  task in the one tree installs its theories only at hand-over and keeps nothing uncommitted there when it parks (q30).
- **Facts in a locale context** reach only the interpretations registered in theories the noting theory imports; facts
  moved above an interpretation are cited as `L.fact[OF <the predicate, stated once>]`; a move of facts probes the
  theories that cite them (review 721's 3).
- **Text with backticks goes through a file**: `v2.py reply`, `tell` and `ledger` given their text as an argument pass
  it through a shell, which strips a backticked name; such a text is written to a draft and passed with `--file`.
- **Size.** A brief's Size is judged against its room with its own relations, the theories it names often the greater
  part; a task beyond its room is handed back at its first start and trimmed to the reasoning that belongs together,
  its corollaries and moves becoming tasks, never by dropping what the kept work must respect.
- **Scope.** Small repairs of the same theories and consolidating follow-ups go into one task up to about half its
  room; a repair another task waits on, or one of a notion's contract, stays its own task. Before briefing a task that
  edits a theory, search `.build/plans/next-edits.md` for it. A task that continues one task's work says `continues`. A
  partial task whose deliverables are written is re-planned, not split; no task rests on reasoning not yet written; a
  fix the planner creates carries no review; a design entry narrowed by its implementation gains a marked correction,
  not a rewrite; a tidy rides with a substantive change of the same theory, never a change of its own.
- **Rebuilds.** A move into a low theory rebuilds nearly the whole library (1798 of 1897 theories, 1770 s; one run
  exhausted Poly/ML's store beside another heavy run, #483's review): low moves are batched into one task, put at the
  queue's head while it checks, so that no other heavy run starts beside it.
