# Reconstruction obligations

This ledger tracks the obligations in `plan.md`. “Proved” names a mathematical
result in the reconstructed theories; it does not close the final alignment or
every-line repository audit. “Revised” identifies a claim whose original form
conflicts with another required distinction. Reasons are recorded in
`DECISIONS.md`. All unlisted higher-layer results from the delivered bootstrap
are historical and are not counted as implementation evidence.

The owner's correction of 2026-09-08 supersedes single-grammar and quotation
principality. O-21 and O-43 through O-45, O-79, and O-80 below use the amended
requirements in the plan. In particular, O-44 and O-80 apply throughout the
system: each admissible presentation must be exact for its notion and its
intrinsic links, and separate exactness results do not establish composition.
The general presentation theory is also required by the owner's subsequent
direction. Its scope follows evidence, and its constructions must be used for
the theory's own relevant notions and throughout the system. O-84 and O-85
track those obligations separately from any individual recovered value.
The remaining audit must record every class, domain, compatibility condition,
linked relation, and corresponding proof. Existing profile-specific recovery
and construction results do not close that system-wide audit.

The owner's further clarification makes notions independent of requirements.
Their domains and intrinsic links belong to local notion or joint-group
contracts. Uses compose those contracts through general laws. Different exact
classes of the same subject domain have a derived semantic correspondence;
they do not require a new meaning-transport proof for each combination.
The remaining audit must enforce this boundary as well as class exactness.
