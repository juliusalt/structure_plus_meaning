# Reasoning that must guide further generalization

## What this document is for

`problems.txt` states the repository's first-use rule: "A generalizable argument must be factored into reusable
content at its first use. A later use should instantiate that content, with its actual prerequisites, rather than
reconstruct the argument." The owner asks the same of every piece of reasoning: "You should never have to use the
same reasoning twice unless the reasoning is truly case specific, non-generalizable"
(`.claude/orchestration/codex-owner-directions.md` §6), and says why it pays: "What we establish about a general
notion and its relationships can settle what would otherwise need to be established separately for many particular
uses" (§4).

This document is the inventory of those arguments. Each row of the table below names one reusable argument, the
premises under which it applies and the conclusion it gives, the native content that states it, and — the column
`problems.txt` cites — what the development does not yet recognize, select or instantiate of it. A further
generalization looks here first and instantiates a row with its actual prerequisites rather than rebuilding it. The
document is generated guidance under the owner's words and `problems.txt`; nothing in it gains authority.

## Standing

The owner's standing rule: "No outside semantics or reasoning or anything else is allowed anywhere. Make this a
standing rule", with the owner's clarification: "Wait isabelle normative bootstrap role is still true until genesis,
this is not what I meant" (codex-owner-directions §10). The owner's direction of 2026-09-19 (the owner ledger;
`DECISIONS.md`, "Native definitions are normative; Isabelle verifies them") places that role: every notion is native
content with native semantics, and "The role of Isabelle is to verify that the native reasoning and definitions are
internally consistent before the native machinery can itself verify this."

A missing use in the fourth column is therefore an implementation gap, never a permitted outside fallback. An
Isabelle proof of a row's contract verifies that the native definition and its reasoning are consistent; it does not
supply a missing native operation, and a HOL function reflected into a native question as a table of its results is
not a native definition of the notion.

## How the inventory is kept

- **A checked notion is cited, never restated.** Where the library states an argument as a checked notion, its row
  names the notion's theory and facts and says nothing the notion states. A use cites the most specific contract that
  states what it needs: an instance's where one states it, the notion's only where none does.
- **A row states no history.** What was built, how it was checked, how many cases ran and where the evidence lies are
  git's, `DECISIONS.md`'s and the validation READMEs'; what one theory offers is its `THEORY_MAP.md` row.
- **The development roles are the plan's.** The contracts of the development's roles — problems and their composition,
  demand, closure, comparison, repair, derived observations, selection, retention, policy and amendment — are traced
  role by role in `native_control_plan.md`, "Existing machinery for each role". Where a row here is one of those
  contracts, its fourth column names the plan's role row and adds only what that row does not say.
- **Choosing an argument is not yet the process's.** Which argument a use instantiates is chosen, for every row, by
  the task that builds the use; the process does not yet select it (the plan's role row "First-use factoring and
  reuse"). The fourth column records what is missing beyond that.
- **Rows move with the library.** A row is added when a landing first makes an argument this document does not state;
  a row whose boundary the library moves is corrected, naming what moved it; an argument the library comes to state as
  a checked notion is from then on cited through that notion.
- **An implementation refinement applies an argument like any other.** A refinement is structural when it applies a
  notion stated where a second use reads it; its acceptance, every report word equal, is a separate condition
  (`DECISIONS.md`, "A refinement applies a notion; an index is one"). The notions refinements apply are rows here.
