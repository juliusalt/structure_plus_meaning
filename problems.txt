The problem I think is that the development process is prose where the system is structure.

The development layer is held as documents, hand-filled tables and bulk artifacts rather than as exact structure with Factor meaning. Three symptoms, one cause:

- Decisions. satisfied_facets_by_candidate is written by the proposer — all the investigations in the current worktree, zero subject contracts. Which problem is taken, which approach is adopted, and whether the constructive exploration runs at all rest on facet satisfactions nobody computed. The thirteen adapters that do have proved index-to-subject equations are not among them: the contracts landed on the layer that does not steer.
- Requirements. DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md is discharged by remembering it. AGENTS.md mandating a full re-read after every compaction is the symptom, not the repair.
- Retention. 11,903,571 lines of validation against 1,529 lines of theory. Reproducible output is stored as bulk rather than as a reconstructible boundary.

The repository's first-use rule: "A generalizable argument must be factored into reusable content at its first use. A later use should instantiate that content, with its actual prerequisites, rather than reconstruct the argument." Enforced on proofs; violated by the process that enforces it, each investigation is identical in structure and rebuilt from scratch.

Conditions any resolution must meet.

1. A decision's facet satisfaction is computed from actual subjects. Where a supplied table remains, its exceptional status is declared and justified against a stated condition, not by preference.
2. A workflow requirement holds by construction. Violating it fails; it does not depend on being.
3. Retained evidence is reconstructible from the repository without storing the reproducible bulk. What is retained is the boundary sufficient to reproduce, not the reproduction.
4. The repeated process pattern is factored at first use and instantiated thereafter.
5. Cost per decision does not scale with accumulated process.
6. The resolution is reached through the process it repairs, or the exception is justified under condition 1.

What does not meet them. A further prose rule, another document, or a stronger instruction — these restate the defect. A metadata dictionary or an added digest. Reducing the number of recorded decisions — that trades quality for throughput, which is not the ask.
