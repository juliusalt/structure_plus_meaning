native reader entries. The [baseline](runs/coverage-before/adequacy.json)
retains eight complete investigations and 29 construction frontiers.
More depth constructs additional binding lists but leaves entries 348–350
unresolved. Their absence is distinct from entry 342's existing application
with unproved source conditions.

constructor problems from the actual library. A proved entry projection

formed entry-level inputs, not admitted environment readings.

definitions and derives their complete premise enumerations. Its first

succeeds; the [duplicate-row case](runs/compiler-duplicate-failure/receipt.json)
throws in the exported singleton selector. The failed run supplies no
accepted semantic result.

compiler reports, 62 intermediate frontiers, and 50 coverage reports.
It retains every schema field and distinct socket. A proved executable
equation accepts every repeated list presentation of a singleton. The
mathematical enumeration and the complete native clause catalog are
unchanged. The final coverage family has 65 complete reports.

sources, accepted and failed proof receipts, checker bytes, execution inputs,
complete results, and the 794-theory main build. Source bytes are shared by

pointing to Poly/ML, and --output naming a new directory. It materializes
the exact required source files unless --project supplies a matching
checkout. It replays the retained cases directly with their original checker
and module. A failure stage succeeds as a replay only when it reproduces the
recorded runtime exception. The replay is validation of existing inputs.

its replay adds that provenance. Earlier reasoning receipts also omitted the
imported build helper. Its retained bytes are unchanged from commit 78af3b0.
