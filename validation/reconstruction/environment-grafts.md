# Complete environment grafts under explicit embeddings

A shared-boundary embedding fixes the supplied boundary use, preserves distinct
use occurrences and places every present imported use outside all reserved uses.
The original long prefix and the compact prefix instantiate this same contract.
The general environment graft preserves every original and renamed artifact and
binding row. Under formed inputs and a shared artifact, its formation is exactly
compatibility of bindings at the shared source. Passive imported boundaries and
unbound literal slots are instances of that remaining condition.

The native comparison derives all observations from complete input environments,
actual mapping graphs and whole output environments. Its four semantic conditions
check boundary identity, finite injectivity, freshness and exact original merge
and rename output. One added natural coordinate is a separate fifth condition.
Every displayed formation result is computed from the actual full environment.
Raw constructor equality does not admit a conflicting or unformed transition.

The family contains ten actual methods and sixteen input pairs. The original,
compact and reversed-suffix compact mappings satisfy the semantic scope; compact
and reversed-suffix mappings also satisfy the coordinate condition. The original
and compact methods have all-input condition proofs; every use of the general
formation theorem retains its original input, shared-artifact and embedding
premises. The reversed-suffix alternative has the stated finite comparison here.
It is not silently identified with suffix-preserving allocation.

Actual cases include old and new source bindings, a compatible active boundary,
a conflicting active boundary, duplicate artifact values, missing shared values,
empty input and a large original environment. The conflict and malformed cases
retain unformed whole results. Omitting bindings, dropping original artifacts or
adding an unsupported artifact fails exact whole-output equality. Colliding and
boundary-changing maps fail their own conditions. With an old word of length 128,
the original map adds 129 coordinates while the compact map adds one.

An empty observation basis has 67 residual pairs and 437 repair witnesses; the
native revision reaches `[3,2,0,1,4]` with an empty residual. The partial and
semantic-only bases retain their own complete native reasons and repairs.
Finite candidate and subject coverage, executable guarded graft admission,
indexed and generation adoption, full physical cost, workflow and genesis
remain open. Reproduction does not discharge these conditions.

report without storing that reproducible output in the repository.
