# Reconstruct nested admission from actual native sources

The problem is to construct an existing, pair or collection requirement over
definitions recovered from the actual source package. The input supplies that
source environment, its package selector and the original goal. It supplies no
separate program model or satisfaction table.

`finite_construct_source_admission` reads the complete native source and checks
every original goal leaf against its definitions. One shared structural
constructor adds the pair and recursive list clauses in those coordinates.
It reuses the original clause families with injectively renamed private
variables and sockets. One checked source extension then compiles the complete
target and returns the correspondingly placed entry.

The universal constructor contract establishes that the returned entry has
exactly the original goal's positive meaning for every term. It also establishes
formed output, the complete original package, and every old artifact and
outgoing binding. The older numbered planner uses the same constructor code
and semantic composition lemmas; its separate counter invariant remains.

Six actual operations are compared: the direct constructor, left projection,
left duplication, removal of collection structure, deletion of an old unused
artifact after construction, and refusal of every request. The 63 problems
combine actual source environments with existing, paired, collected and nested
goals, including an absent requested definition. Term samples come from the
goal's shape and a fixed original term family, without consulting candidate
truth values. Empty clause families, recursive clauses, literal targets,
different component meanings, an empty package, an absent package and an unused
malformed artifact remain in the source family.

The conditions concern soundness and completeness against the original goal,
complete preservation of old material and bindings, and refusal of unsupported
requests. Term comparison requires native source recovery, supported entries
and a successful exact finite evaluation of both programs. Missing evaluation
produces no term comparison. The returned extra and missing terms have an exact
contract with the original positive meaning.

`Finite_Subject_Investigation` supplies the common subject maps and observation
equation. `Finite_Assessed_Investigation` shares each actual assessment across
all facets and comparisons. Adequacy requires every represented condition,
including a condition omitted from a sufficient comparison basis. The runtime
reads the checked contract to obtain its actual candidate and facet domains.

The complete execution selects the direct constructor alone, and it alone is
adequate. Projection and duplication expose wrong positive terms, missing
positive terms and accepted unsupported requests. Removing collection structure
exposes both extra and missing terms. Deleting the unused artifact leaves entry
meaning intact while failing preservation on six problems. Refusing every
request fails on all 36 supported problems.

With no selected facets, 22 comparisons remain unresolved; the computed
revision selects preservation, soundness, completeness and refusal. Soundness
alone leaves six unresolved comparisons, and its revision adds preservation
and refusal. Reinvestigating both returned selections leaves no residual.
The full four-facet selection is unchanged. All original requirements remain
part of adequacy regardless of which basis distinguishes this finite family.

The reconstruction compares 131 complete reports: one scope, 63 original
subjects and outputs, 63 assessments with counterexample terms, one comparison
and adequacy result, and three complete investigation and revision reports.
