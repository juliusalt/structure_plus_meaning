# Reconstruct requirement families over actual native sources

The problem is to construct one native entry requiring every original admission
goal of the same term. The source environment, package selector and complete
ordered goal family are the inputs. The operation recovers the actual program;
no separately supplied program model or satisfaction table supplies its meaning.

`finite_construct_source_requirements` checks every original leaf against that
source. The shared stateful traversal constructs each goal in the current
program, retaining an indexed result for every original occurrence. The final
guard reuses the original conjunction schema. Distinct private socket positions
retain repeated occurrences even when they call the same definition. The empty
family admits exactly formed terms. One final source compilation places the
complete target and returns its corresponding entry.

The universal contract establishes the original same-term conjunction for every
term, complete source agreement, formed output, the original native package,
and every old artifact and outgoing binding. Separate occurrence and schema-field
contracts retain the complete ordered construction and socket family. The older
numbered sequence planner now uses the same executable traversal, combining its
returned instruction chunks in order. Single goals and families share actual
source recovery, construction, placement and preservation proofs.

The term observer also shares one source-reading and evaluation construction.
A family test requires term formation and each original goal at that same term.
Comparison with the returned entry reports every extra and missing tested term.
Its exact condition includes actual source recovery, original leaf support,
returned-entry membership and available exact evaluation of both programs.
Unavailable evaluation supplies no negative judgment about a term.

The investigation instantiates the shared subject maps, assessment, comparison,
adequacy and revision operations. Ninety problems combine nine actual source
cases with ten original families: empty, singleton, repeated, distinct, paired,
collected and nested goals, and missing leaves in both positions. Samples come
from the original goal shapes and a fixed term family, without reading candidate
truth values. They include a malformed payload. An empty program, an absent
package, an unused malformed artifact and a valid unused artifact distinguish
source readiness and whole-environment preservation.

Eight actual operations retain the family, drop its first goal, drop its last
goal, repeat its first goal at every position, remove an old unused artifact,
return no construction, reject empty families, or append an extra paired goal.
The extra goal pairs the first original goal with itself; empty families stay
unchanged. The four independent conditions
require soundness and completeness with evaluable programs, complete preservation,
and rejection of unsupported original requests. Adequacy retains all four
conditions even when fewer observations distinguish these particular operations.

The complete execution returns 186 reports and 2,597 satisfied observations out
of 2,880 candidate-condition-problem positions. Only the direct constructor is
selected and adequate. It constructs all 49 ready requests and rejects the
remaining 41. Dropping or repeating requirements admits extra terms and can
accept a request after removing its unsupported leaf. Removing the unused
artifact fails only preservation in cases 80 through 87, despite exact term
meaning. Returning no construction fails the three positive obligations on all
ready requests. Rejecting empty families fails those obligations on all seven
ready empty-family cases, including the empty source program.

The operation that adds a requirement returns an evaluable construction on all
49 ready requests. It fails only completeness, with nonempty missing-term sets
in 31 cases. In case 1 it excludes the pair of two empty payloads, which satisfies
the original diagonal requirement. In cases 41 through 43 it excludes both
material target forms and the two payload terms. The complete missing-term
families also cover nested pairs and collections. All original methods' failure
groups and repair-witness multiplicities remain unchanged.

The empty family accepts all six formed terms in its fixed sample and rejects
the malformed payload. The repeated family in case 3 retains two distinct native
guard sockets calling the same original definition. Case 50 constructs the empty
family over an empty recovered program. These structural and boundary
checks accompany the semantic comparison; equal term masks alone would not
establish occurrence retention.

Starting with no observed conditions leaves 45 unresolved comparisons. The
returned revision selects `[3,0,1,2]` and its subsequent investigation has none.
Starting with soundness alone leaves fifteen unresolved comparisons; the returned
`[0,3,1,2]` basis removes them. Starting with all four conditions leaves none and
retains the basis. Complete profiles, losses, repair witnesses and followed
results are reproduced, including the 1,639 available repairs from the empty
basis and 253 from soundness alone.

The preceding seven-operation investigation had no available comparison with a
nonempty missing-term set. Its completeness failures came from absent
construction. This criticism supplied the extra-requirement operation before
executing the expanded investigation. The earlier returned basis `[0,3,2]`
leaves six unresolved comparisons: methods 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 against method 7.
Each comparison has the same 31 completeness witnesses, for 186 repairs. The
computed revision retains the original observations and adds completeness,
returning `[0,3,2,1]`; its subsequent investigation has no residual comparison.
Adequacy requires all four original conditions throughout this process.

These results establish the stated finite comparison. Further candidate and
problem coverage still requires its own investigation.
Repeated subgoals can also generate separate intermediate definitions; this
result does not establish a minimal construction or the complete execution-cost
account. The constructor's universal meaning proof and the finite investigation
have their stated, different domains.

A source review found a provisional filename collision with the earlier numbered
requirement cases. Their original bytes were restored, and the new family cases
now have a separate theory. The combined proof includes both older source and
extension clients. The earlier 63-case single-goal investigation reproduces all
131 reports exactly after the shared construction and observation changes.

--output <fresh directory>`. Without `--proof`, it rebuilds the complete theory
closure from HOL, exports the checked implementation and reproduces the complete
reports. The source descriptor and report boundary retain the inputs needed for
that reconstruction; generated proofs, modules and logs are reproducible outputs.

These results do not establish arbitrary-method coverage, general finite
program decision, admission and enforcement of the complete development record,

final repository audit. Isabelle retains its normative bootstrap role through
