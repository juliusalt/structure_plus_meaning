# Recorded causes bound to their original policy

The original complete native source and requirement family construct the
policy before record admission. Its actual package site, complete dependency
scope and called entry must agree with the recorded judgment. The original
certified-base-cause check remains separate. An all-input theorem establishes
that every admitted record satisfies every original requirement under its
original source meaning.

The native fixture has a nonempty requirement family. Its actual source clause,
constructed guard, derived certificate, closed replay and generation record
precede each method assessment. Ten complete cases expose substitution of the
original policy, a wrong entry, changed whole program material, a missing proof,
unavailable original requests, and shifted or reordered cause quotations.
Eight methods instantiate the shared optional-family assessment. Native
selection and adequacy retain only the exact gate.

The complete original requirement decision is retained independently. It refuses
the literal payload under the rejecting source and is unavailable for malformed
or unsupported requests. It permits the payload in several cases where the
record fails the stricter policy-identity or replay condition. These are
separate judgments; payload permission alone establishes neither identity nor
a permissible alternative policy realization.

reader result, independent decision and certificate family, computed condition,
comparison and revision. This scoped gate does not establish adequate policy
for every development task, enforcement of every workflow action, historical
permission, unrestricted criticism coverage, physical cost or genesis.
