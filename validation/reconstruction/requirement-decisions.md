# Reconstruct original-requirement decisions

One operation constructs a required entry from the complete actual native source,
reads its installed program, derives its finite demand from the original terms,
and generates the complete positive answer and certificate family. The source
constructor's independent entry contract determines the returned qualifying terms.
The requirement instance constructs every original requirement and their final
conjunction. Failure of any original requirement excludes that term.

A result retains the actual entry, complete extending environment, source use,
installed program, term-derived demand, all positive calls and certificates, and
the admitted original terms. Every positive call has a certificate, and every
certificate passes the original source checker. An available empty answer
remains distinct from an unavailable source, unsupported requirement or failed
finite evaluation. No supplied satisfaction table enters this calculation.

Eight independent conditions inspect original precision and completeness, full
source preservation, actual installed-program recovery and exact demand,
complete finite positive answers, sound complete certificates, exact target-term
projection and refusal when the original input is unavailable. Each observation
has the complete original subject contract. The independent original source
and requirement evaluation remains in the result alongside the candidate's
actual source reading, applications, rules, answer and every proof-check value.

The execution retains twelve complete original inputs and twelve actual methods:
1,152 condition positions, of which 978 hold and 174 fail. Only method 0 is
selected and adequate. Omitting or duplicating a requirement can coincide with
the original decision on some inputs, but differing original requirements
expose the error. Removing an unsupported requirement returns an answer where
the original operation is unavailable and fails refusal. An unused source
artifact makes complete preservation independently observable.

Erasing a claimed program, demand, answer or certificate family produces
separate failures. Sound certificates do not validate an erased demand or a
changed answer. Dropping all admitted terms passes precisely when the original
admitted family is already empty. Certificates for positive original source
calls remain present even when no target term satisfies all requirements.
Every full subject, result, inspection, profile, directed loss and repair field
was reviewed. Both incomplete observation bases are repaired by the native
cycle; all three followed comparisons are formed without residuals.
